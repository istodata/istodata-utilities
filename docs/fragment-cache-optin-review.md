## Exact stop finding

The EN desktop hit request had zero loops, zero original dropdown Template
renders and one live outer mega-menu render. Its real heavy dropdown control was
e-n-menu-dropdown-icon-6532, controlling e-n-menu-content-6532.
After click and 700 ms, aria-expanded was false rather than true. The browser
assertion failed. No success claim for the EN interaction.
The EN script, unlike the GR script, did not explicitly await mouseover handler
registration. A timing issue and a real missing initialization/replay remain
possible; neither cause has been proved.

Proposed correction: focused uncached versus hit EN comparison after waiting for
actual Elementor handler registration. If uncached opens and hit alone fails,
repair asset/init replay. If both initially fail before registration, correct
the test synchronization. Do not certify the candidate merely by increasing a
sleep. Then repeat the full still-pending acceptance.
